FOR MUM & DAD

Teaching together, one day at a time.

Suggested daily routine

  1. 10 min · Warm-up and multiplication practice
  2. 15 min · Explain the lesson
  3. 10 min · Work through an example together
  4. 20 min · Independent worksheet
  5. 5 min · Mark and review

12-week course plan

Each workbook contains six units, with roughly two weeks per unit. Use the workbook’s review quiz to decide whether to move on or practise again.

Tip: The workbooks include answer keys. For independent tests, parents should supervise access to those pages.
